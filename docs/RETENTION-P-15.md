# P-15 retention and PM ruling (15 November 2026)

Cron `bin/cron.php` logs deletion counts without personal data; direct output to a private, rotated log. All intervals are positive configurable seconds in `retention`: OTP challenges 86400 from creation but never while unexpired; expired sessions 2592000 after expiration; OTP idempotency metadata 2592000 from creation; undeliverable SMS 7776000 from creation. Failed-delivery OTP challenges are retained until both OTP and undeliverable intervals pass. Pending sends are never deleted automatically because their outcome can be in flight or uncertain. A key with a surviving challenge is retained. Existing OTP replay data is migrated separately from challenge secrets; duplicate/conflict behavior survives challenge cleanup for the key window.

The existing schema has no separate disposable undeliverable-SMS table. Confirmed announcements and all SMS delivery requests (including uncertain and accepted) are retained indefinitely under the ruling; neither is pruned. Replication batch fingerprints and all audit records are never pruned. Before final launch, retained personal data must be reviewed for minimization by the owner/legal adviser; that review is outside P-15.
